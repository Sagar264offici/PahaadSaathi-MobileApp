# PahaadSaathi — Community Ecosystem & Help Exchange

---

## 1. Geographical Structure
The community model is structured specifically around Uttarakhand's administrative divisions:
- **Districts:** Chamoli, Rudraprayag, Uttarkashi, Pithoragarh, Tehri Garhwal, Pauri Garhwal, Dehradun, Bageshwar, Almora, Nainital, Champawat, Haridwar, Udham Singh Nagar.
- **Tehsils & Blocks:** E.g., Joshimath, Karnaprayag, Ukhimath, Bhatwari, Dharchula.
- **Gram Panchayats & Wards:** E.g., Mana, Bhyundar, Triyuginarayan, Harsil.

---

## 2. Help Exchange Workflows
- **Categories:** Food, Drinking Water, Shelter, Transport (4x4 Bolero / Utility), Medical Assistance, Rescue Support.
- **Status Progression:**
  `open` → `in_progress` (volunteer assigned) → `fulfilled` → `closed`
- **Abuse Prevention:** Posts require local community association; emergency hotlines are cached locally.

---

## 3. Verified Volunteer Network
- **Skills:**
  - `first_aid`
  - `transport` (mountain vehicles)
  - `local_route_knowledge` (foot trails and bypasses)
  - `shelter_assistance`
  - `food_distribution`
  - `water_distribution`
  - `rescue_support`
- **Verification:** Volunteers require administrative verification before earning the `Verified Volunteer` badge; civilian volunteers are explicitly distinguished from professional SDRF/NDRF rescue teams.
